# Talks

This repo grew out of two talks. The slides are not published; the guide is the up-to-date version of what they said.

1. [Agentic workflows](2026-07-exotea.md), ExoTea, July 2026: one agent per project, CLAUDE.md, the vault, my first skills and hooks.
2. [Working with agents](2026-11-llm-discussion.md), LLM Discussion Series, November 2026: the vault as shared memory, the MARVEL orchestrator, and the cockpit.

Agents were "future work" in July. By November an orchestrator was running the MARVEL pipeline. The gap between the two talks is most of what [the guide](../guide/README.md) describes.
